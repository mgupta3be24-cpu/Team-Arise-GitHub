## 🎯 About the Project

The Smart Lab Management & Continuous Evaluation System aims to simplify the management of laboratory activities and academic records.

The system provides a centralized platform for organizing student information, managing teams, maintaining laboratory records, and supporting attendance and continuous evaluation.

It is intended to reduce manual record keeping and make laboratory-related information easier to manage.

## ✨ Features

- **Student Management:** Maintain and organize student records.
- **Team Management:** Organize students into teams and groups.
- **Laboratory Activities:** Maintain records related to laboratory activities.
- **Attendance Management:** Support attendance tracking.
- **Continuous Evaluation:** Organize marks, feedback, and evaluation records.
- **Excel Import:** Support importing structured student or team information through Excel files.

*Feature availability should be verified against the current application.*
