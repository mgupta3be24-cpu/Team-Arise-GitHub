# 🚀 Team Arise

## Smart Lab Management & Continuous Evaluation System

A centralized platform designed to simplify laboratory record keeping, student management, and continuous evaluation.

[🌐 **Open Live Application**](https://software-9c1b8.web.app/)

---

## 📌 About the Project

The Smart Lab Management & Continuous Evaluation System aims to organize laboratory activities and academic records in one place. It focuses on making student information, team records, attendance, and evaluation easier to manage.

## ✨ Core Modules

### 👨‍🎓 Student Management
Organize student information and maintain student records.

### 👥 Team & Group Management
Manage student teams and organize students into groups.

### 📋 Lab Activities
Keep laboratory activities and related academic records organized.

### 📊 Attendance & Continuous Evaluation
Support attendance tracking and the management of marks and feedback.

### 📂 Excel Import
Use Excel files to import student or team information, according to the application's supported format.

---

## 🛠️ Technology Stack

- **Frontend:** Flutter / Dart
- **Backend & Database:** Firebase / Cloud Firestore
- **Data Import:** Excel

*Confirm these technologies against the final implementation before submission.*

## 🔄 Project Workflow

1. Organize student information.
2. Create and manage teams or groups.
3. Maintain laboratory activity records.
4. Record attendance and evaluation information.
5. Access the information through the application.
