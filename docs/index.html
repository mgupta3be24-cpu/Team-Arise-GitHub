One platform for laboratory records

Project overview

Laboratory courses involve managing student details, teams, groups, activities, attendance, marks and feedback. When information is spread across separate spreadsheets and registers, maintaining consistent records can become difficult.

Team Arise is developing a centralized application to organize these workflows and reduce repetitive administrative effort. Excel-based data import is intended to simplify the entry of student and team information.

The project is structured to support future improvements, including student access, online submissions and reporting enhancements.

🎯

Project objectives

Centralize records, simplify team management, organize attendance and evaluation, and provide a foundation for future expansion.
